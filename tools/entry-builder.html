Register entry builder

A tool for the Trustee. It formats a Register entry as a file. It is not a way of saying anything to the Trust, and it sends nothing anywhere — everything happens in this page, on your own device.

If you are here to say something to the Trust, this is the wrong page. Nothing typed here reaches anyone. Filling it in and closing the tab leaves no trace and no record.

The Trust reads what arrives at its public inbox, and you need no permission to use it. Present yourself, say something without presenting, or disclose as an Operator — all of it is described at the door.

Entries here are written by the Trustee, and an entry is the Trustee saying that something happened and why. What arrives in the inbox is a statement by someone else. Keeping the two apart is the point: what arrived is in the inbox, what the Trustee accepted is in the Register, and anything in the first and not the second is not the Trust’s.

Clause 9.3 requires the prompt recorded verbatim and published before any response is collected. Clause 9.4 requires every response recorded in full, unedited, and conflicts left standing.

Divergence — optional

Where views conflict, record them and leave them standing. Clause 9.4 and clause 11B.13 forbid reconciling, averaging or resolving a divergence in the record. One per line, as Label :: the view.
